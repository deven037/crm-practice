SLA, ticket edit and ticket record: loading, empty and error states

Scope of AIO-377: SLA settings, edit ticket and ticket record, plus one shared error banner with Retry. Board and requester-list states stay on AIO-348 (PR #91). Same Harbor tokens as AIO-327.

Shared error banner

Red banner, role="alert", a bold title, one line of help, and a 44 px Retry button. Retry keeps its place and becomes “Retrying…” with a spinner (disabled) until the request ends; a second failure restores “Retry”. A 404 shows “not found” with no Retry; a 5xx or network error shows Retry. Copy: “This ticket didn’t load”, “SLA hours didn’t load”, “Ticket not found”.
