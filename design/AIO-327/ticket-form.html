New ticket

Starts as Open. The due time follows the priority’s SLA hours.

Fix the 2 fields below to create the ticket.
Subject is required.
Requester is required.
Medium: due 24 hours after creation.
Custom fields
Format: WT-12345-0101
Cancel

States

Saving

Button is disabled and reads “Creating…” with a spinner. Typed values stay.

Save failed

One banner above the buttons; nothing typed is cleared and Create ticket is enabled again, so pressing it retries (AC22).

The ticket wasn’t created. Check your connection and try again.
Cancel

Created

Opens the new record with a toast: Ticket “Wire transfer stuck in pending” created.

Rules and hooks

Errors show after Create ticket is pressed or a field is left, tied to the field with aria-describedby. Custom-field rule errors use the same style and block saving. Viewers don’t get this page: “+ New Ticket” is disabled for them. Hooks: ticket-form-page, ticket-subject, ticket-requester, ticket-priority-select, ticket-description.
