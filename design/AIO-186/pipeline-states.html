Deals: states

What the pipeline shows when it is loading, empty, filtered, failed or read-only. Header and actions stay in place in every state.

Loading

Skeleton columns match the board, so nothing jumps when data arrives. Announced as “Loading deals”.

Empty pipeline

No deals at all. The page explains what a deal is and offers the one action. Viewer sees the sentence only: “Deals will appear here once your team adds them.”

No deals in your pipeline yet

Add a deal to track it from Qualification through to Closed Won. Every stage then shows its count and total.

Empty column and filtered view

An empty column replaces “Drop deals here” with guidance that works without a mouse. “Showing open deals” hides both Closed columns (the Dashboard link) and says so, with a way back.

Responsive

At 768 the shell becomes the overlay and columns scroll inside the board. At 390 each state fills the width with 16 px gutters; banners stack and Retry becomes full width, 44 px tall.

Bank theme

Empty-state and Retry buttons use --bank-primary and --bank-on. Banners use fixed tone tokens, so a bank cannot make an error look like success.
