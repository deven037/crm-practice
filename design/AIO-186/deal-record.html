What the bank changes

Only --bank-primary (#0f5c5c, 7.8:1 against its white --bank-on) and the name. It fills the completed steps, the connector lines, the current-step ring and the tide line above the app. The name stays on --text; the stage pill keeps its fixed warning tone so colour never decides meaning.

Stepper

An ordered list, not a decoration: completed steps carry a tick, the current one aria-current="step", and every state also has a text caption. Closed Won or Lost replaces the last label with that value. Moving stages happens in Edit or on the board, so the stepper stays read-only.

Fields and fallbacks

Account and Linked quote are links. A missing account, owner or quote shows “—”. Configured custom fields (Fleet size, Contract term are samples) follow Created, as today.

Roles

Admin and rep: Edit and Delete. Viewer: neither button; everything else identical. Edit turns the details into the form with Cancel and Save (see dialogs screen).

Responsive

1440: details left, activity right. 768: one column. 390: the stepper stacks vertically, labels and values stack, actions share a row at 44 px.
