How it works

Moving a deal

Drag still works with a mouse. Every card also has a Move button (arrow icon, 44 px on phone) that opens a menu of stages, so keyboard, touch and screen-reader users never need to drag. Choosing a stage moves the card, updates both column totals and announces the toast above.

Reading a column

The total sits under the stage name; the thin bar and “% of open value” show how much of the open pipeline the column holds. Closed columns carry a text tag (“Won”, “Lost”), so stage never relies on colour alone.

Roles and bank

Admin and rep: New Deal, Move, edit. Viewer: no New Deal and no Move; cards open the record read-only. A bank changes only --bank-primary, --bank-on (New Deal fill, share bars, probability bars, sidebar line). The record screen shows a sample bank.

Responsive

1440: five columns fit. 768: navigation is the overlay (780 px breakpoint); columns scroll sideways inside the board only. 390: one column at a time, 84% wide, snapping, so the next stage peeks in; the page itself never scrolls sideways.

Keyboard order

Board/List switch, New Deal, then per column: each deal title, then its Move button. Escape closes the Move menu and returns focus to its button.

Test hooks kept

deals-page, kanban-board stay on the same elements. Cards open the existing edit dialog (see dialogs screen).
