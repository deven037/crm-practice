How it works

Sample bank

Shown with Northquay (--bank-primary #0f6b5c, white text). It re-themes the logo tile, sidebar edge, SLA bar, and the “Add comment” fill. Priority and status pills keep their meaning colours and text.

Same as the reading pane

This page is also what a phone user sees after choosing a ticket in the inbox: “‹ Tickets” goes back to the list. Content and actions match the pane (AC5).

SLA states

Shown: past due, red bar and “SLA breached”. Active: “3h 12m left” (h m s ticking) with a bank-colour bar. Resolved or Closed: “SLA not applicable (ticket isn’t active)”, no bar. Screen readers hear changes once a minute and “SLA breached” once (AC32); the visible countdown still ticks each second.

Move to

Only allowed moves show: Open → In Progress, Closed. In Progress → Resolved, Open. Resolved → Closed, In Progress. Closed → Open. (Verified: TICKET_TRANSITIONS, server/src/types.ts.)

Narrow screens

1024: details stack above/below, single column. 390: single column; SLA and details come after description; buttons and selects 44 px; comment box and Add comment full width.
