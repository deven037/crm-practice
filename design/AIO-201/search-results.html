Design decisions

  • Why each row matched: the matched characters are bold with a bank-accent underline, in the name, company, email or phone line, so nobody has to read a row to see why it is there.
  • Layout: a 640 px panel anchored under the field, 52 px rows, a 32 px type tile, and the total for each type in its heading ("12 matches"). "See all 12 leads" appears only when a type has more than 3 matches, and it is a real option in the arrow-key order.
  • Current row: bank-tinted fill, a 3 px accent bar and a solid type tile. Keyboard highlight and mouse hover use one rule for the fill; only the keyboard-highlighted row gets the bar and tile, so colour is never the only signal.
  • No clash with other header controls: the panel hangs under the 60 px bar, left-aligned to the field, and never overlaps the theme, notification or user buttons. The Esc hint and clear button live inside the field. Opening the panel does not move any other control.
  • Role rules (R6): where a role has no Email/Phone permission, rows show without that text and the term is not matched against it. Types the role cannot open are absent, and the footer total counts only what that user may see.
  • Tokens: every colour comes from the Harbor variables; dark uses the [data-theme='dark'] values from src/styles.css. Muted text in dark is lifted to #a3abbd so it keeps 4.5:1 on the panel. The bank primary fills the highlighted tile and the "See all" text; its lighter accent is used where it sits on dark.
  • Motion: the panel opens with a 120 ms fade and 4 px rise; with reduced motion it just appears.
  • Phone: the panel spans the full width under the bar, rows are 56 px, the clear button is 44 px, and "Cancel" replaces the header icons while searching.
