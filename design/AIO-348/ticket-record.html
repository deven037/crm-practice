How it works

Sample bank

Northquay (--bank-primary #0f6b5c, white text) re-themes the logo tile, sidebar edge, SLA bar and primary buttons. Priority and status keep their meaning colours.

Same body as the inbox pane (AC5)

Identical sections and order as the pane in tickets-inbox, at record width. Shown with the first comment in edit mode: the text box keeps the comment, Save and Cancel replace Edit and Delete. Phone users reach this page from the inbox and use “‹ Tickets” to return.

Hooks on existing elements

ticket-priority is the editable select (as in today’s TicketReadingPane), not a badge. There is no separate priority badge. ticket-status is the status pill. sla-countdown exists only while Open or In Progress and not yet breached; the three SLA states are rendered in ticket-states.

Move to

Only allowed moves show: Open → In Progress, Closed. In Progress → Resolved, Open. Resolved → Closed, In Progress. Closed → Open (TICKET_TRANSITIONS).

Narrow screens

1024: same single column, 960 px max. 390: title above status and Delete; meta fields stack; buttons and fields 44 px; Add comment full width.

Other states

Loading, not found, SLA active/inactive, viewer, every failed save and the two delete dialogs are in ticket-states.
