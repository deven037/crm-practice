Build notes for Alex

  • Layout: split screen from 768 px up (brand 5fr / form 4fr); under 768 px the brand side is hidden and a compact product mark plus bank name sits above the form. Form is 380 px max, 20 px gutters on a phone.
  • Product name from the VITE_PRODUCT_NAME token (as Dashboard does), bank name and logo from VITE_BANK_NAME / VITE_BANK_LOGO_URL. The brand side fills with --bank-primary; all text on it uses --bank-on. Tide shapes are inline SVG filled with --bank-on at 5–12% opacity.
  • Primary button fills --bank-primary with --bank-on text, 48 px high. Inputs 46 px. Show-password toggle sits inside the field (42 px target).
  • Keep every data-testid (login-page, login-email, login-password, login-submit, login-error, reset-error), the validation in loginValidation.ts, and role="alert" on banners. Add aria-invalid and aria-describedby as today.
  • Danger text #b91c1c on #fef2f2 and on white both pass 4.5:1. Placeholder #6b7280 on white is 4.8:1. Muted text #5b6472 is 6:1.
  • Transitions 120 ms on border, shadow, button; none under prefers-reduced-motion.
